## Content Templates

Every post should follow this structure for maximum AI extractability:

### Post Structure

```
1. Opening paragraph: Direct answer to the target query (40-60 words, self-contained)
2. Context/Problem: Why this matters (2-3 paragraphs)
3. Framework/Solution: Structured with H2s that match query patterns
4. Practical examples: Real decisions from your own work
5. Data points: At least 2-3 statistics with sources
6. FAQ section: 3-5 questions phrased as people actually search
```

### Frontmatter Template

```yaml
---
title: "[Exact target query or close variation]"
description: "[50-160 chars, includes target query naturally]"
excerpt: "[Quotable one-liner that works as a standalone statement]"
publishedAt: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: ["relevant", "tags"]
keywords: ["target query", "variation 1", "variation 2", "related term"]
featured: true/false
draft: false
targetQuery: "exact query you want to rank for"
socialTitle: "[Optimized for social sharing, can differ from title]"
socialDescription: "[Optimized for social, more casual/compelling]"
---
```

## Publishing Cadence

- **Target:** 2 posts per month minimum
- **Quality bar:** Every post must have: 1 self-contained definition paragraph, 2+ data points with sources, 1 FAQ section with 3+ questions
- **Update cycle:** Revisit and update posts every 6 months (add `updatedAt` to frontmatter)

## Monitoring Checklist (Monthly)

1. Search your target queries in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google
2. Record: Are you cited? Who is cited instead?
3. Track which posts get the most organic traffic
4. Update any posts older than 6 months with fresh data
5. Check Google Search Console for new query opportunities
